Update to Census Data Releases

The recent government shutdown has delayed Census Bureau data releases for the 2024 American Community Survey (ACS). The new release dates are:

  • December 4, 2025: 2024 ACS 1-year PUMS Files and 2024 ACS 1-year Supplemental Estimates
  • January 29, 2026: 2020-2024 ACS 5-year data and 2020-2024 ACS 5-year Variance Replicate Estimates
  • March 5, 2026: 2020-2024 ACS 5-year PUMS Files

New release dates for the vintage 2024 estimates from the Population Estimates Program (PEP) are:

  • January 27, 2026: Total Population and Components of Change Estimates for the Nation, States, and Puerto Rico; Voting-age Population Estimates for the Nation, States, and Puerto Rico Commonwealth
  • March 26, 2026: Total Population and Components of Change Estimates for Counties, Puerto Rico Municipios, and Metropolitan and Micropolitan Statistical Areas
  • April 9, 2026: Population Estimates by Age and Sex for the Nation
  • May 14, 2026: Total Population Estimates for Incorporated Places and Minor Civil Divisions; Housing Unit Estimates for the Nation, States, and Counties

These delays impact the release of SDO data products that rely on the ACS 5-year estimates. Updates to the HNA data bundles, the ACS spreadsheet series, the CSBG data bundles, and the revised Colorado Demographic Profiles bundles will be delayed until the beginning of March 2026.

State Demography Summit held on  November 7, 2025

The 43rd State Demography Summit was held on November 7, 2025 at the Arvada Center.  Over 400 people participated in this annual event. Summit materials are available on the SDO website. Video of the summit presentations will be posted in the near future.

SDO Discusses HNA data bundle at DOLA Housing Strategies Peer Exchange, January 15, 2026, 9:00 a.m.

SDO will discuss its new HNA data bundle tool during DOLA’s Monthly Housing Strategies Peer Exchange meeting on January 15, 2026, at 9:00 a.m.

The Community Development Office is hosting a monthly peer exchange series for local governments working on affordable housing strategies and land use issues. This interactive virtual series will take place on the third Thursday of each month from 9:00 to 10:00 a.m.

The intent of the series is to provide a forum for sharing challenges, successes, and innovative approaches, and to learn from peer communities across Colorado who are implementing solutions to local housing challenges. Each month will feature a different topic with brief presentations from selected Colorado communities, with ample time allowed for questions and discussion.

Joe Davidson Joins the State Demography Office

Joe Davidson joined SDO November 12 as the new Program and Data Assistant supporting outreach, data collection, and office operations. Joe previously worked at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory as a web and analytics manager, and at Colorado PERA as a communications specialist.
