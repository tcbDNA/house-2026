(NewsNation) — The race for the soon-to-be open Senate seat in Alabama was another test of President Donald Trump’s endorsements, as U.S. Rep. Barry Moore takes on Jared Hudson. 

Ahead of the primary election last month, Trump endorsed Moore during a tele-rally. Moore and Hudson advanced to the runoff election.

Decision Desk HQ projected Tuesday that Moore prevailed against Hudson to claim a spot in November’s primary election against Everett Wess, who DDHQ projected to beat Dakarai Larriett.

The Alabama Senate seat will be vacated by U.S. Sen. Tommy Tuberville, who is not seeking reelection and is instead running for governor. 

Moore and Wess will face off in the general election this November.
