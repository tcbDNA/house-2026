How this was counted

The count follows Alaska law (AS 15.15.350) and the rules on the Division's results page:

  • One candidate, the one with the fewest votes, is eliminated each round, until two remain or someone has a round-1 majority.
  • Write-ins are left unresolved: a write-in ranking is passed over, without counting as a skip.
  • A ballot becomes inactive when it reaches an overvote, or after two skipped rankings in a row.
  • A candidate ranked more than once counts only at the highest ranking (6 AAC 25.072).
  • The statewide pages show every round, even where the scanned ballots alone give someone a round-1 majority.
