Florida Poll Finds Donalds +2, Inside Margin Of Error

October 29, 2026 News, Politics

Florida Politics reports:

Republican Byron Donalds leads Democrat David Jolly 48% to 46% in a new St. Pete Polls survey of the Governor’s race for Florida Politics. It is the first time in three surveys by the pollster that either candidate has led by more than a tenth of a point. The sample assumes an electorate 12 points more Republican than Democratic. Ballots cast through Tuesday morning are less than a point apart.

Another 3% picked someone else and 3% are undecided. The margin of error is plus or minus 3.2 percentage points, and Donalds’ lead is inside it. Republicans hold a registration advantage of roughly 1.5 million voters over Democrats, nearly 5.69 million to almost 4.15 million at the Oct. 5 book closing, with another 3.35 million registered to no party. That is R+8 on the rolls. The poll’s sample is R+12.
