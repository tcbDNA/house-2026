Congressional District GA-01 Info

There are 582,343 registered voters in GA-01. Nobody is running as an incumbent for this district.

Congressional District GA-01 Voting

172,589 people have voted so far in the 2026 general election, representing 29.6% of registered voters.
The breakdown is 8,891 mail votes, and 163,698 early, in-person votes.

175,124 people have requested a ballot for the 2026 general election.

White voters are 62.5% of the early vote so far, down from 65.4% at this point in 2022 and 67.1% in 2024.
