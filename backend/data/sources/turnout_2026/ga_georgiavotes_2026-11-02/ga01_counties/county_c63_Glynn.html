Glynn County Voting

24,499 people in Glynn County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 36.1% of the county’s registered voters.

1,366 voted by mail and 23,133 early in person.

Turnout so far is 8% higher than at this point in 2022, when 22,632 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 74.1% of the early vote so far, down from 76.7% at this point in 2022 and 76.0% in 2024.

Glynn County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Glynn County’s two-party margin was R+26.1, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 104th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Glynn County.

Glynn County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 26.7 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 23.9 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Glynn County Info

With 87,599 residents, Glynn County is the 31st most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 67,948 registered voters, the 30th most of any county.
