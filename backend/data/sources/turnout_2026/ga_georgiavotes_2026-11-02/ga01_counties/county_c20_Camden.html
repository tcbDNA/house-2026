Camden County Voting

12,839 people in Camden County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 29.3% of the county’s registered voters.

755 voted by mail and 12,084 early in person.

Turnout so far is 17% higher than at this point in 2022, when 10,956 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

Hispanic voters are 2.3% of the early vote so far, up from 1.7% at this point in 2022 and 2.6% in 2024.

Camden County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Camden County’s two-party margin was R+35.9, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 89th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Camden County.

Camden County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, and the gap has widened from 30.7 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 33.7 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Camden County Info

With 60,143 residents, Camden County is the 41st most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 43,772 registered voters, the 39th most of any county.
