Ware County Voting

5,987 people in Ware County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 26.8% of the county’s registered voters.

397 voted by mail and 5,590 early in person.

Turnout so far is 8% lower than at this point in 2022, when 6,515 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 66.4% of the early vote so far, down from 69.5% at this point in 2022 and 72.8% in 2024.

Ware County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Ware County’s two-party margin was R+43.3, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 73rd most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Ware County.

Ware County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 42.6 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 41.1 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Ware County Info

With 36,906 residents, Ware County is the 55th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 22,322 registered voters, the 65th most of any county.
