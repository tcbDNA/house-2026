Long County Voting

2,855 people in Long County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 21.9% of the county’s registered voters.

80 voted by mail and 2,775 early in person.

Turnout so far is 16% higher than at this point in 2022, when 2,465 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 49.4% of the early vote so far, down from 58.9% at this point in 2022 and 60.9% in 2024.

Long County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Long County’s two-party margin was R+29.6, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 98th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Long County.

Long County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 29.4 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 27.4 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Long County Info

With 21,557 residents, Long County is the 85th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 13,045 registered voters, the 97th most of any county.
