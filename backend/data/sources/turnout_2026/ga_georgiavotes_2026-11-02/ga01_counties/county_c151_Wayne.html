Wayne County Voting

6,234 people in Wayne County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 30.3% of the county’s registered voters.

231 voted by mail and 6,003 early in person.

Turnout so far is 12% higher than at this point in 2022, when 5,590 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 76.2% of the early vote so far, down from 79.0% at this point in 2022 and 81.5% in 2024.

Wayne County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Wayne County’s two-party margin was R+59.9, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 30th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Wayne County.

Wayne County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 60.0 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 57.7 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Wayne County Info

With 32,481 residents, Wayne County is the 62nd most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 20,582 registered voters, the 69th most of any county.
