McIntosh County Voting

3,737 people in McIntosh County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 36.0% of the county’s registered voters.

143 voted by mail and 3,594 early in person.

Turnout so far is 2% higher than at this point in 2022, when 3,660 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

Black voters are 26.2% of the early vote so far, down from 26.6% at this point in 2022 and 23.0% in 2024.

McIntosh County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, McIntosh County’s two-party margin was R+28.7, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 101st most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is McIntosh County.

McIntosh County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, and the gap has widened from 18.3 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 26.5 points in the 2024 presidential race.

McIntosh County Info

With 7,676 residents, McIntosh County is the 141st most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 10,383 registered voters, the 111th most of any county.
