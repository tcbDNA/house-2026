Chatham County Voting

66,716 people in Chatham County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 28.9% of the county’s registered voters.

3,962 voted by mail and 62,754 early in person.

Turnout so far is 17% higher than at this point in 2022, when 57,088 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 53.2% of the early vote so far, down from 54.7% at this point in 2022 and 57.8% in 2024.

Chatham County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Chatham County’s two-party margin was D+18.2, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 13th most Democratic of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Chatham County.

Chatham County has voted more Democratic than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, and the gap has held steady: 20.6 points in the 2018 governor’s race, 20.4 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Chatham County Info

With 311,855 residents, Chatham County is the 5th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 231,094 registered voters, the 5th most of any county.
