Brantley County Voting

2,966 people in Brantley County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 23.2% of the county’s registered voters.

78 voted by mail and 2,888 early in person.

Turnout so far is 1% lower than at this point in 2022, when 3,000 had voted.

White voters are 92.9% of the early vote so far, down from 94.5% at this point in 2022 and 93.6% in 2024.

Brantley County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Brantley County’s two-party margin was R+82.6, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 2nd most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Brantley County.

Brantley County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 82.3 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 80.4 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Brantley County Info

With 18,936 residents, Brantley County is the 99th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 12,803 registered voters, the 100th most of any county.
