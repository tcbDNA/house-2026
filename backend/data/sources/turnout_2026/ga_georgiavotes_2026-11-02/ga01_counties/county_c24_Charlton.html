Charlton County Voting

1,814 people in Charlton County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 25.1% of the county’s registered voters.

81 voted by mail and 1,733 early in person.

Turnout so far is 2% lower than at this point in 2022, when 1,849 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

Black voters are 18.9% of the early vote so far, down from 21.6% at this point in 2022 and 15.9% in 2024.

Charlton County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Charlton County’s two-party margin was R+56.4, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 34th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Charlton County.

Charlton County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, and the gap has widened from 49.6 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 54.1 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Charlton County Info

With 13,121 residents, Charlton County is the 112th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 7,225 registered voters, the 119th most of any county.
