Appling County Voting

3,786 people in Appling County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 30.1% of the county’s registered voters.

209 voted by mail and 3,577 early in person.

Turnout so far is 4% higher than at this point in 2022, when 3,657 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 75.6% of the early vote so far, down from 78.7% at this point in 2022 and 79.9% in 2024.

Appling County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Appling County’s two-party margin was R+62.5, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 27th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Appling County.

Appling County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, and the gap has widened from 58.6 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 60.3 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Appling County Info

With 19,100 residents, Appling County is the 98th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 12,576 registered voters, the 102nd most of any county.
