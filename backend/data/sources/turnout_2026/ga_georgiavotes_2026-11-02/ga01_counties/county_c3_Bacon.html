Bacon County Voting

2,550 people in Bacon County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 36.1% of the county’s registered voters.

82 voted by mail and 2,468 early in person.

Turnout so far is 7% lower than at this point in 2022, when 2,743 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 85.8% of the early vote so far, down from 89.6% at this point in 2022 and 88.4% in 2024.

Bacon County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Bacon County’s two-party margin was R+73.3, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 8th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Bacon County.

Bacon County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 72.9 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 71.1 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Bacon County Info

With 11,070 residents, Bacon County is the 121st most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 7,056 registered voters, the 121st most of any county.
