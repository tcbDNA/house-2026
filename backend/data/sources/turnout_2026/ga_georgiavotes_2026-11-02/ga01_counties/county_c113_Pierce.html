Pierce County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Pierce County’s two-party margin was R+77.7, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 5th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Pierce County.

Pierce County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 77.1 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 75.4 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Pierce County Info
