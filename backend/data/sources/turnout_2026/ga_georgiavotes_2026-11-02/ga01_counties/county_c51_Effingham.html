Effingham County Voting

13,999 people in Effingham County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 26.2% of the county’s registered voters.

517 voted by mail and 13,482 early in person.

Turnout so far is 8% higher than at this point in 2022, when 12,989 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 70.5% of the early vote so far, down from 75.4% at this point in 2022 and 75.7% in 2024.

Effingham County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Effingham County’s two-party margin was R+49.3, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 52nd most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Effingham County.

Effingham County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 54.1 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 47.1 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Effingham County Info

With 74,397 residents, Effingham County is the 34th most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 53,416 registered voters, the 34th most of any county.
