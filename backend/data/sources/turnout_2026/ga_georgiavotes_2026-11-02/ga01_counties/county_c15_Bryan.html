Bryan County Voting

12,304 people in Bryan County have voted early in the 2026 general election, 32.5% of the county’s registered voters.

350 voted by mail and 11,954 early in person.

Turnout so far is 7% higher than at this point in 2022, when 11,470 had voted.

Votes so far each day, against 2022 at the same number of days before election day. Drag the slider to see the whole voting period.

White voters are 70.8% of the early vote so far, down from 75.0% at this point in 2022 and 74.4% in 2024.

Bryan County Election History

In the 2024 presidential election, Bryan County’s two-party margin was R+36.5, compared with R+2.2 statewide. That made it the 87th most Republican of Georgia’s 159 counties.

Every Georgia county, sized by its 2024 presidential vote; Democratic-leaning on the left. The marker is Bryan County.

Bryan County has voted more Republican than Georgia in all nine statewide contests since 2018, though the gap has narrowed from 40.4 points in the 2018 governor’s race to 34.3 points in the 2024 presidential race.

Bryan County Info

With 52,062 residents, Bryan County is the 42nd most populous of Georgia’s 159 counties. It has 37,894 registered voters, the 42nd most of any county.
