## Figures as printed

Turnout = ballots cast by registered party members over that party's registered voters. Transcribed from the Issue #81 table (2012–2022); 2024 from the Issue #234 text ("Democrats achieved 78.8% turnout to the Republican's 85.5%").

| General election | DEM | GOP | NPA | Total | D v R gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | 72.0% | 78.0% | 61.1% | 71.5% | -5.9% |
| 2014 | 50.1% | 60.4% | 37.7% | 50.4% | -10.3% |
| 2016 | 74.2% | 81.1% | 64.2% | 74.0% | -6.9% |
| 2018 | 64.4% | 71.0% | 48.1% | 62.2% | -6.5% |
| 2020 | 77.2% | 83.8% | 66.8% | 76.7% | -6.5% |
| 2022 | 52.1% | 66.9% | 38.6% | 53.5% | -14.8% |
| 2024 | 78.8% | 85.5% | — | — | -6.70% (chart) |

Caveats from the posts: the 2022 party figures were collected from county turnout trackers and are called unofficial (Issue #81). MCI's later final 2022 report (Issue #97, https://mcimaps.substack.com/p/issue-97-final-2022-florida-turnout) divides by active plus inactive voters and gets D 49% and R 63%; the table above uses the registration counts printed in it. No 2010 figure was found.

The state's own turnout pages give totals only, not party: https://dos.fl.gov/elections/data-statistics/elections-data/voter-turnout/ and the general election summaries at https://dos.fl.gov/elections/data-statistics/elections-data/general-election-surveys/ (ballots by type).
